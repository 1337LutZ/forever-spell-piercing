How Spell Piercing works

Spell Piercing doesn't add a flat amount of damage. It is the other side of the partial resist roll: each hit or tick at the full share rolls a step, +0%, +10%, +20%, and at high values +30%. In the combat log the bonus shows up as a negative resisted number. Most DoT ticks roll no bonus at all.

  1. Debuffs like Curse of the Elements lower the target's resistance, but never below 0. A debuff that would take it lower adds nothing.
  2. Spell Piercing comes off what's left. Once it passes 0, the rest turns into bonus damage. Against a target with no resistance, all of it is bonus on a hit at the full share.
  3. The average share is R / (|R| + 8 × your level + 19), where R is the resistance after debuffs minus your piercing. A positive share is resisted damage, a negative one is bonus damage.
  4. Up to an average of 10%, each hit lands on 0, 10 or 20% at 1 − 7.5a, 5a and 2.5a. Past 10%, every 10% step within 20 points of the average can come up, at 0.5 − 2.5 × its distance from the average.
  5. No partial resist takes more than 75%: the 80, 90 and 100% steps land on 75%. A spell that isn't binary is never fully resisted through this roll.

The share depends on your level, not the target's, so the same piercing is worth less as you level. Holy has no resistance in Forever and takes no piercing.

Hit types

  • Direct, channel or full-share tick: rolls the full share and takes the piercing bonus in full. Fireball, Scorch, Pyroblast, Fire Blast, Shadow Bolt, the Arcane nukes, Lightning Bolt, Starfire, Wrath, Blizzard's ticks, and the ticks of Devouring Plague, Pyroblast and Starshards.
  • DoT tick (tenth): rolls a tenth of the share, and only when the target still resists, so it takes no piercing bonus. The sim lets piercing still lower the target's resistance first; the logs haven't measured that yet. Most DoT ticks: Shadow Word: Pain, Corruption, Flame Shock, Moonfire, Fireball's DoT, and Flamestrike's ground ticks.
  • Binary: a magic damage spell that has, besides the damage, a debuff on the target, an interrupt, or a dummy, script, energize or area-trigger effect. The average share is the chance to resist the whole spell, and one that lands takes no partial resist. Piercing lowers the chance of a full resist down to 0, and past that the hit takes the bonus like a direct hit. Frostbolt, Frost Nova, Cone of Cold, Ice Lance, Frostfire Bolt, Blast Wave, Arcane Wrath, Flamestrike's cast, Immolate, Incinerate, Shadow Word: Death and Earth Shock. Binary ticks split: Mind Flay's and Insect Swarm's take the bonus at the full share, Hurricane's, Entangling Roots', Bane of Agony's and Drain Soul's take none.

What the logs show

  • 15 piercing at around level 22, almost 3,000 hits on the beta realms: +10% came up exactly twice as often as +20%, for an average bonus of 7.5%. Every school takes it, and crits roll the same as normal hits.
  • A Fireball log with more piercing shows the +30% step once the average passes 10%.
  • Since build 71037 (19 November) binary hits take the bonus at the full rate: Frostbolt 1,863 of 6,276 hits (1,802 expected), Cone of Cold 1,111 of 4,675, Arcane Wrath 898 of 3,385, Flamestrike's cast 994 of 3,235, Mind Flay's ticks 483 of 5,398, Insect Swarm's 1,089 of 9,240. Before that build they took none.
  • Ticks at a tenth take none: Corruption, Siphon Life, Wrack, Shadow Word: Pain, Flame Shock and Moonfire at a few percent of the full rate at most, Flamestrike's ground ticks 7 of 2,454, a level 60 warlock with 11 piercing 0 of 512. Hurricane's ticks took 19 of 7,577 where the full share expects 786.
  • No live hit loses more than 75%, and on the mobs that resist that much, 75% is the top step.
  • A caster with 1 piercing got no bonus in 1,791 hits, where the formula expects a few percent.
  • The 8 × level + 19 constant is fitted to logs from levels 20 to 50, among them 15 piercing at level 50 (5,756 hits, 26% with a bonus). Level 60 is the model's prediction until someone logs it.
